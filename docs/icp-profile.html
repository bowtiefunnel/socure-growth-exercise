Buyer-Persona Matrix

CRO

Chief Risk Officer
Owns
Fraud-loss P&L; approve/decline policy
KPI drivers
Fraud-loss bps · false-positive ratio · synthetic-ID capture · manual-review volume
Pain Socure targets
Rising synthetic & first-party fraud; good users declined; review teams that don't scale

CCO

Chief Compliance Officer
Owns
KYC/CIP, AML, sanctions, audit defensibility
KPI drivers
CIP auto-approval/pass rate · watchlist coverage · SAR quality · exam outcomes
Pain Socure targets
CIP failures, manual remediation, multi-vendor audit trails, BSA/AML exposure

VP Product / Growth

Conversion owner
Owns
Onboarding conversion; activation
KPI drivers
Auto-approval rate · onboarding step-up friction/drop-off · time-to-yes
Pain Socure targets
Declined-good-applicant = lost LTV; friction kills the funnel

CTO / Head of Eng

Platform owner
Owns
Integration, uptime, orchestration cost
KPI drivers
API latency/SLA · single-integration consolidation · config-change velocity
Pain Socure targets
Stitching point vendors; slow rule changes; brittle in-house logic → the RiskOS pitch
